# Area51 Lock

Bringing the personal back to your PC.

Area51 Lock adds passcode protection to the browsers and desktop apps you choose.
Lend someone your computer without leaving every application open to them.

## Download and install

1. Open the [latest release](https://github.com/conceptztech/area51/releases/latest).
2. Under **Assets**, download the file ending in **Setup.exe**.
3. Run the installer from your normal Windows account, not as administrator.
4. Read and accept the license and terms, then finish installation.

The installer includes the required .NET runtime. You do not need to install it separately.
GitHub's **Source code** ZIP and TAR.GZ downloads are not installers.

## Get started

1. Open Area51 Lock and verify your email address.
2. Create a passcode using numbers, letters, symbols or a mixture.
3. In **Unprotected**, choose **Set guard** beside the apps you want to protect.
4. Close the dashboard to leave Area51 running in the notification area.

When you open a protected app, enter your passcode to access it. Your existing windows
are hidden while locked and restored when you unlock. Protected apps relock when your
display turns off, your PC sleeps or your Windows session locks.

## Made for everyday use

- Protect browsers and other supported desktop applications.
- Manage apps in **Protected**, **Unprotected** and **Hidden** lists.
- Select several apps to apply bulk actions.
- Use **Lock apps now** to lock all protected apps together.
- Hide apps from the lists without changing their protection.
- Recover a forgotten passcode through your verified email address.

Area51 is an application-window guard, not file encryption or a replacement for Windows
sign-in. Keep it running for protection and keep Windows security enabled.

## Privacy

Passcodes and app preferences stay on your PC. Unlocking works offline. Area51 does not
collect browsing history or use tracking analytics.

Email verification, passcode recovery and account deletion use the online recovery service.
The app also connects to GitHub to check for signed updates. Keep access to your recovery
mailbox outside the apps you protect.

## Updates

Area51 checks for signed desktop releases and notifies you when an update is available.
You can install it immediately. After a 72-hour grace period, Area51 attempts automatic
installation while it is running and online.

This repository hosts official installers, update manifests and release notes. It does not
contain the application's development source code. The `area51-update.json` release asset
is used by the updater; you do not need to open it yourself.

## Uninstalling

The uninstaller offers a normal uninstall that keeps your local profile for reinstalling,
or a complete clean uninstall that removes Area51's local profile. Neither option deletes
your online registration. To delete that registration, use **Settings → Delete account**
inside Area51.
